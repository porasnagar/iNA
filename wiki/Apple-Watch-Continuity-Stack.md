# Apple Watch Continuity Stack

iNA integrates Apple Watch (Series 4 through Series 10, Apple Watch Ultra 1 & 2, and SE) with Android by hosting on-device GATT peripheral servers and handling Apple Continuity services.

---

## 1. Apple Notification Center Service (ANCS)

The Apple Notification Center Service (ANCS) enables an accessory (the Apple Watch) to retrieve notifications generated by a smart device (the Android phone).

### Service Architecture
- **ANCS Service UUID**: `7905F431-B5CE-4E99-A40F-4B1E122D00D0`
- **Notification Source Characteristic**: `9FBF120D-6301-42D9-8C58-25E699A21DBD` (NOTIFY)
- **Control Point Characteristic**: `69D1D8F3-45E1-49A8-9821-9BBDFDAAD9D9` (WRITE)
- **Data Source Characteristic**: `22EAC6E9-24D6-4BB5-BE44-B36ACE7C7BFB` (NOTIFY)

### Flow Diagram
```text
Android System Notification
           │
           ▼
iNA NotificationListenerService
           │
           ▼
[EventID: Added, CategoryID: Email/Message/Call, NotificationUID]
           │
           ▼ (Sent via Notification Source)
Apple Watch displays notification banner
           │
           ▼ (Optional user tap to read details)
Apple Watch sends [GetNotificationAttributes] via Control Point
           │
           ▼
iNA responds with [Title, Message, Date, App Identifier] via Data Source
```

## 2. Apple Media Service (AMS)

The Apple Media Service (AMS) allows the Apple Watch to control audio playback on Android via the Digital Crown and touchscreen controls.

### Service Architecture
- **AMS Service UUID**: `89D3502B-0F36-433A-8EF4-C502AD55F8DC`
- **Remote Command Characteristic**: `9B3C81D8-57B1-4A0F-B867-0E05342F5B6F` (WRITE / NOTIFY)
- **Entity Update Characteristic**: `2F7DD41B-30EA-43AE-9D5B-8CD599E26FDA` (WRITE / NOTIFY)
- **Entity Attribute Characteristic**: `C6B2F38C-23AB-46D8-A6AB-A3A870BBD5D7` (READ / WRITE)

### Supported Media Controls
- Play / Pause / Toggle
- Next Track / Previous Track
- Volume Up / Volume Down (Digital Crown rotation)
- Track Title, Artist Name, and Duration synchronization to watch face

## 3. Health & Sensor Telemetry

When connected, iNA reads real-time health sensor characteristics exposed by the Apple Watch GATT server:
- **Heart Rate Service**: UUID `0x180D` (Characteristic `0x2A37` - Heart Rate Measurement)
- **Battery Service**: UUID `0x180F` (Characteristic `0x2A19` - Battery Level)
